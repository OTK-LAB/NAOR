# NAOR - Requirements

## 1. Project Organization
- `Assets` folder must be clean.
- Separate Art (Sprites, UI), Scripts (Gameplay, UI, Systems), Prefabs (NPCs, UI, Environment).
- Resolve loose scripts and prefabs at the root of the `Assets` folder.

## 2. Refactoring & Optimization (Milestone 1)
- Identify unnecessary abstractions or dependencies and remove them.
- Optimize the execution speed and cleanup legacy code.
- YAGNI principle: Build only what is necessary, keep it simple and straightforward.

## 3. UI/UX (Milestone 2)
- Overhaul the Main Menu.
- Provide a clean, robust Menu architecture.

## 4. Scene & Mechanics (Milestone 3)
- Combine the two existing gameplay scenes into a unified experience.
- Understand, discuss, and refine current mechanics.
- Develop and integrate the story within the gameplay.
