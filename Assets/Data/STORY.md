# NAOR: Lore & Story Outline

## Core Conflict
The realm of NAOR is fractured. The Nine Aspects of Realms (9 Aspect of Realms) have been shattered by a rogue Necromancer. The protagonist, a lone soul-warrior armed with simple tools (bombs, daggers, swords), must venture through the fractured lands to reconstruct the Aspects.

## NPCs & Integration Points
- **Fetch NPCs (FetchNPC1, FetchNPC2):** Survivors of the shattering. They request specific lost items in exchange for permanent stat boosts (HP, Mana, Ability Power).
- **The First Boss:** Guards the entrance to the inner realm, corrupted by dark magic.

## Atmosphere
Dark, desolate, but with sparks of hope. The dialogue should be minimal, punchy, and melancholic.
